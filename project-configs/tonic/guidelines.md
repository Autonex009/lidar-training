# Overview

- These instructions define how to annotate objects in LiDAR point cloud
  data using 3D cuboids (bounding boxes). Annotations must be complete,
  consistent, and tightly fitted to the object in every frame.

- The ontology covers different object classes across different groups:
  person, Dynamic Objects, and Static Objects. Every visible object from
  this list within a radius of 10m must be annotated in every frame
  where it appears.

- The goal of this module is to train annotators to **accurately
  identify and label** the dynamic objects like Human**,** Humanoid
  Robot, Forklift, Pallet Jack, Cart, AGV / AMR, Wheelchair, Vehicle and
  Static objects like Pallet, Tote, Bin, Rack, Shelf, Conveyor,
  Workstation, Table, Chair etc. with high consistency.

# General Instructions

- Draw 3D bounding boxes (cuboids) around all objects from the label
  list, and assign the correct label.

- Do not apply more than one cuboid to a single object.

- Check every cuboid in every frame to ensure all LiDAR points are
  contained within the cuboid.

- The cuboid center must be the geometric center of the entire object,
  including parts not currently visible.

- Cuboid orientation: the length axis must align with the object's
  direction of travel; the height axis points upward; the width axis is
  perpendicular to both.

- Object IDs must remain consistent across all frames in which the
  object appears. Do not switch IDs between objects.

- IDs must be unsigned integers (e.g. 0, 1, 2). The order they are
  assigned is not important.

- The cuboids should be properly aligned with the object in the 2D
  image. Please ensure the 3D cuboid fits the visible 2D object
  correctly.

# Required fields per object

Every cuboid must have:

- Track ID (persistent across frames, never reassigned within a
  sequence/camera)

- Class ID: one of the 22 ontology classes (Human, Humanoid Robot,
  Forklift, Pallet Jack, Cart, AGV/AMR, Wheelchair, Vehicle, Pallet,
  Tote, Bin, Rack, Shelf, Conveyor, Workstation, Table, Chair, Charging
  Station, Dock Door, Safety Barrier, Machines, Boxes)

- Occlusion state:

  - None = >90% visible

  - Partial = 30-90% visible

  - Heavy = <30% visible

- Motion state: Static / Moving (present on every class)

**Pre-labelled cuboids**

The pre-labels are cuboids generated by a model to assist with the
annotation process. While working on the tasks, annotators should
carefully review each pre-label and check whether it is relevant to the
objects covered under their respective branch.

- **If the pre-label is relevant:** Review the cuboid carefully, change
  its class to the appropriate ontology/class, and make sure it is
  properly aligned with the object across every frame.

- **If the pre-label is not relevant:** Delete the pre-label and
  continue annotating the other relevant objects in the task.

Please make sure that all pre-labels are reviewed rather than being
accepted as-is

**Regions of interest**

- ROI 1 (0-5): needs to be perfect quality (5% error)
- ROI 2 (0-10): everything labelled
- ROI 3 (>10 m): good but optional if lidar points are bad

# Human

> A person present in the scene, in any posture or activity (Standing,
> Walking, Running, Sitting, Kneeling, Bending, In-Wheelchair/ADA,
> etc.). Cuboid covers the full expected body volume, including any
> occluded portion.
>
> Cuboids shall encompass the expected physical body volume including:

- Head

- Torso

- Arms

- Legs

> For partially occluded humans, annotators shall estimate the hidden
> body portion and maintain a physically representative cuboid.
>
> Cuboids shall not be reduced solely to fit visible pixels.

# Humanoid Robot

> A bipedal, human-shaped robot performing tasks in the facility (e.g.
> picking, walking). Not a person - a robot built with a human-like body
> plan.
>
> For articulated poses, the cuboid shall represent the overall occupied
> space of the robot.
>
> ![Humanoid robot standing in a lab, bipedal human-shaped body](images/image9.png)

# Forklift

> A powered industrial truck with a fork/mast assembly used to lift and
> move palletized loads. Cuboid includes chassis, operator compartment,
> and forks.
>
> Cuboids shall encompass:

- Chassis

- Operator compartment

- Integrated fork assembly

> ![Yellow forklift with mast, operator compartment and forks](images/image20.png)

# Pallet Jack

> A low-profile manual or powered truck with two forks that slide under
> a pallet to lift and move it short distances. Lower and smaller than a
> forklift; no raised mast.
>
> Cuboids shall encompass:

- Pallet footprint

- Attached load when permanently associated with the pallet

> ![Yellow manual pallet jack with two forks and a handle](images/image15.png)

# Cart

> A wheeled, hand-pushed or hand-pulled cart used to carry goods, tools,
> or totes (e.g. a push cart or hand truck). Not motorized and not a
> pallet jack.
>
> ![Black two-tier utility push cart](images/image31.png)![Red flat-bed platform cart with push handle](images/image29.png)

# AGV / AMR

> An Automated Guided Vehicle or Autonomous Mobile Robot: a driverless,
> self-navigating robotic vehicle that moves goods without an onboard
> human operator.

![Side-by-side comparison of an AMR (left) and an AGV (right)](images/image3.png)

# Wheelchair

> A wheeled mobility device. Annotate the device itself with this class;
> if a person is using it, also annotate the person as Human with
> activity state In-Wheelchair/ADA.
>
> ![Empty manual wheelchair](images/image27.png)

# Vehicle

> Any motorized vehicle which doesn’t belong to the other mentioned
> category.
>
> ![Car body frames on an assembly line with orange robot arms (Vehicle example)](images/image18.png)

# Pallet

> A flat wooden or plastic transport structure used as a base for
> stacking goods. Cuboid covers the pallet footprint plus any load
> permanently attached to it.
>
> ![Stacks of empty wooden pallets](images/image35.png)

# Tote

> A rigid, reusable container used to hold and transport smaller items,
> per the customer's tote/bin class reference. Not a loose cardboard
> box.
>
> ![Blue rigid totes stacked on a blue pallet](images/image23.png)![IBC-style rigid container in a metal cage on a pallet](images/image5.png)

# Bin

> A rigid, reusable storage container, distinct from Tote per the
> customer's class reference and distinct from Boxes (which are
> non-rigid/disposable).
>
> ![Three wheeled waste bins (glass, paper, plastic) in a row](images/image7.png)

# Rack

> A multi-level storage structure that holds palletized or shelved
> goods. Annotate each rack section individually - never combine a full
> aisle into one cuboid.
>
> ![Aisle of tall pallet racking with blue uprights and orange beams](images/image28.png)

# Shelf

> An individual shelving unit or level used to store smaller,
> non-palletized items (distinct from a full Rack structure).
>
> ![Multi-level shelving unit with blue uprights and wooden shelf boards](images/image4.png)

# Conveyor

> A belt, roller, or powered system used to move goods along a fixed
> path.
>
> ![Conveyor lines with totes and cartons, robot arms alongside](images/image10.png)

# Workstation

> A fixed work area or setup (e.g. a packing or sorting station) where a
> task is performed.
>
> ![Workstation with benches, pegboard tool panels, monitor and cabinets](images/image8.png)

# Table

> A general-purpose flat-surface furniture item, not part of a dedicated
> workstation.
>
> ![Flat-top work table with lower shelf in a warehouse](images/image33.png)

# Chair

> A seating furniture item.
>
> ![Rolling stool with round green seat and footring](images/image12.png) ![Four black stackable metal chairs](images/image19.png)

# Charging Station

> Fixed equipment used to charge batteries for equipment such as
> forklifts, AGVs/AMRs, or other powered vehicles.
>
> ![Row of upright EV-style charging stations on wheeled bases in a clean facility](images/image2.png)

# Dock Door

> A loading dock door or bay opening used for loading/unloading
> trailers.
>
> ![Row of numbered loading dock doors](images/image25.png)

# Safety Barrier

> A physical barrier (railing, bollard, guard) installed to protect
> people, equipment, or defined zones.
>
> ![Yellow and black safety barrier railing and bollards along a walkway](images/image13.png)

# Machines

> Industrial machinery in the scene that does not fit any other class.

> ![Stainless-steel industrial machine with control panel](images/image22.png)

# Boxes

> A loose cardboard box or shipping carton (not a rigid reusable
> container). Cuboid includes any lid/flap overhang; annotate
> stacked-but-separable boxes individually, not as one stack cuboid.
>
